Researched 2026-08-18. Sync licensing (лицензирование треков для рекламы/кино/ТВ/игр) is a separate
channel from the streaming distribution work in [[project_music_distribution_djlevka]] — no conflict between
them, they run in parallel. See also [[project_wikipedia_notability_lev]] for the 10-release discography this
applies to.

**Lev's position: "one-stop" artist.** No label or publisher — he owns 100% of both master and publishing
rights himself, which is the most attractive artist type for sync agencies/music supervisors (no third-party
split negotiation needed).

**Genre fit is a real advantage:** Funky House / Nu-Disco / Afro House is one of the more in-demand sync
genres — upbeat, instrumental-forward, fits lifestyle/fashion/travel ad placements and corporate/tech promo
reels well.

**Platform landscape (verified 2026-08-18):**
- **Songtradr** — non-exclusive marketplace, self-serve (no curation gate), artist keeps ownership. 60% cut on
  free tier, 80% on Pro. Recommended entry point — can upload the catalog immediately.
- **Marmoset** — boutique agency, ~50/50 splits, hand-picked "story-driven" placements. Good genre fit but
  selective, not self-serve.
- **Musicbed** — non-exclusive but <1% acceptance rate, applications open only twice a year. Aspirational
  future target once the catalog is larger, not now.
- **Music Vine** — 60% on exclusive tracks / 35% on non-exclusive.
- **Epidemic Sound / Artlist — CAUTION:** buyout/exclusive models. Epidemic Sound pays a $2,000–8,000 upfront
  fee per track but it's a rights buyout (signs over a share of ownership), not a license. Don't put strong
  singles already running through Our Angels/Sundesire/Beatport-chart strategy into these — only consider for
  minor/throwaway instrumental tracks, if at all.

**Two RU-specific obstacles found:**
1. **Payout rails** — same pattern as the streaming-distributor research: most sync platforms pay via
   PayPal/SWIFT wire, both broken for RU residents since 2022 sanctions. Must be verified per-platform via
   direct inquiry before submitting (same method used successfully with Our Angels — anonymous support inquiry
   before giving personal data). Crypto wallets (e.g. Kraken, which declared neutrality) surfaced as a
   workaround in sources but carries added legal/practical risk for a minor's account — not a first-choice
   move.
2. **PRO/performance-royalty gap** — a sync placement that airs on broadcast (TV/cable) normally also earns
   recurring performance royalties via a PRO (RAO in Russia), separate from the one-time sync fee. PRS (UK),
   ASCAP, and BMI formally cut ties with RAO in March 2022 over sanctions; CISAC did not expel RAO but left the
   decision to each national society, so most international royalty flow through RAO is effectively cut. The
   one-time sync fee itself (paid directly by the licensing platform/agency) is unaffected — but the
   recurring broadcast performance-royalty tail, which for a national TV ad can exceed the sync fee itself, is
   likely uncollectable via the standard RAO route right now. Not a blocker to starting sync work, just a gap
   to revisit if/when a real broadcast placement happens.

**Update 2026-08-18: Lev already has a RAO contract/membership** (user confirmed, exact terms not yet
verified — likely standard author registration for RU domestic performance/broadcast royalty collection,
not a one-off per-project sync license; confirm if wrong). This closes the domestic side: RU radio/TV/public-
performance royalties are already routed through existing infrastructure, nothing new to set up there. It does
**not** close the international gap described above — the PRS/ASCAP/BMI severance from RAO is an
inter-society agreement break, not tied to any individual member's contract status, so a foreign broadcast
sync placement (US/UK TV etc.) still likely won't flow performance royalties back through RAO regardless of
Lev's own membership.

**RAO IPI numbers received 2026-08-24:** Lev's CISAC IPI (Interested Parties Information) identifiers, sent by
RAO — **IP Base Number: I-007496433-1**, **IP Name Number: 01327314376**. IPI is the global rightsholder-ID
system shared by all CISAC-member PROs worldwide (ASCAP, BMI, PRS, GEMA, etc.), not RAO-specific — confirms Lev
is formally/internationally registered, not just nominally affiliated. Practical use: these numbers will likely
be requested when setting up composer/publisher accounts on sync platforms (Songtradr, Musicbed, Marmoset) or
with any future foreign sub-publisher. **Does not change the international-collection-gap finding above** — IPI
is an identification/matching system for works, not a payment channel; having a valid IPI lets a foreign PRO
like ASCAP correctly match a work to Lev, but whether royalties actually flow back through RAO still depends on
the bilateral society agreement (severed since 2022), independent of IPI registration status.

**Contract actually read 2026-08-24 (5-page scan, signed 2025-11-05) — corrects and extends the above:**
- **Territory is contractually worldwide, not RF-only.** п.2 has both а) РФ and б) «всех стран мира» signed — the earlier framing of "domestic collection closed, international separate" was imprecise. RAO is contractually authorized (п.3) to pass management to foreign author-rights societies and collect on Lev's behalf globally. The PRS/ASCAP/BMI severance (see above) is a constraint on which foreign societies RAO can actually still work with, not a limitation written into Lev's own contract.
- **Potential direct conflict with the sync-licensing plan.** п.1 подпункт ж) is signed: "воспроизведение музыкальных произведений... в составе аудиовизуальных произведений для использования всеми способами, предусмотренными действующим законодательством" — reproduction of music within audiovisual works, worldwide. п.7а) obligates the Author not to grant this same right (for the methods in п.1, territory in п.2 — i.e. worldwide) to any third party while this contract is active. This is close enough to what sync licensing does (placing music into video/ads/film) that it may conflict with independently listing the catalog on Songtradr/Musicbed/Marmoset. **Needs clarification from RAO or a lawyer before proceeding with sync-platform signups** — this is a new blocker/open question for CH.3, not previously known.
- п.1 подпункт з) (визуальное/декоративно-прикладное искусство, фото — распространение/показ/загрузка) was explicitly **refused** (handwritten "ОТКАЗ") — RAO has no mandate over Lev's photography/visual art, only music-related categories. Not relevant to DJ Levka's music but worth knowing the boundary.
- Contract term: 2025-11-05 to 2026-12-31, auto-renews yearly unless either party sends written notice (registered mail, delivery confirmation) at least 30 days before the period ends. Not open-ended — revisit around Nov/Dec 2026.
- Payment: RUB only; RF-sourced ≥4x/year, foreign-sourced ≥2x/year (as received by RAO), net of taxes and RAO's own fee/fund deduction (rate not stated in the contract, set unilaterally by RAO's internal acts).
- Signing this contract voided any prior agreement between the same parties.
- RAO requisites: ИНН 7703030403, ОГРН 1027739102654, ул. Большая Бронная 6а стр.1 Москва, email rao@rao.ru.

**Registry registration gap found 2026-08-25:** the contract's п.1 grants blanket coverage a priori — "произведения,
созданные им как до заключения договора, так и в период его действия" (past and future works, no resigning
needed). But that's only the *legal scope*; actual royalty collection requires each work to be separately
registered in RAO's Реестр (п.7д). Per the contract text (page 3): if a work isn't registered in time, RAO has
no obligation to collect for it, and no retroactive recalculation happens once distribution has occurred. User
needs to check the rao.ru personal cabinet (Личный кабинет правообладателя) and register any of the 10 releases
that are missing — confirmed not all are currently there. Full contract scan saved locally at

**How to apply:** Start with Songtradr (self-serve, non-exclusive, no gatekeeping) — verify their RU payout
method via direct support inquiry first, same approach as Our Angels. Avoid Epidemic Sound/Artlist for
catalog-strength tracks. Keep Musicbed/Marmoset in mind for later once the release catalog is bigger. If a
real broadcast sync placement materializes, separately research alternative performance-royalty collection
routes given the RAO/CISAC gap — don't assume RAO will pay out.
